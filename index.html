About ih5z

Small edits, big moods.

ih5z is a one-person music edit project. Slowed + reverb, sped up, remixes, mashups -- whatever the mood calls for. Requests keep the project alive.

Privacy notice

When you submit a request, the fields you fill in are sent to ih5z to consider. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Your submission is never displayed publicly -- the song, artist, and edit style stay between us.

Requests are considered, not guaranteed.
